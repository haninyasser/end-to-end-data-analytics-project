# End-to-End Data Analytics Project

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end data analytics project based on the Northwind dataset. It covers the complete data analysis workflow, starting from data understanding and cleaning and progressing through data transformation, SQL analysis, business intelligence, visualization, and strategic insights.

The project focuses on analyzing sales performance, profitability, customers, products, employees, categories, and shipping-related information to identify key business problems and provide data-driven recommendations.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- **Excel** – Data understanding, cleaning, transformation, Power Query, Power Pivot, and Pivot Tables
- **SQL Server** – Data analysis and querying
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboards and business intelligence
- **Tableau** – Data visualization and dashboard development

---

## Project Workflow

The project follows an end-to-end analytical workflow:

**Data Understanding & Cleaning → Data Transformation → SQL Analysis → Power BI → Tableau → Insights & Recommendations**

Each stage builds on the previous one to transform raw data into meaningful business insights.

## Key Business Findings

The analysis identified several important business areas, including:

- Overall sales and profitability performance
- The impact of freight costs on profitability
- The effect of excessive discounts on profit margins
- Underperforming and loss-making products
- Customer contribution to total sales
- Opportunities for improving overall business performance

Detailed insights and strategic recommendations are available in the **Insights** section.
