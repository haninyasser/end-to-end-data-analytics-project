# Key Insights & Strategic Recommendations

## 1. Understanding the Data Context

- **Project Overview:** This project analyzes data from a commercial company specializing in food and beverage products, similar to international sales and distribution companies operating across Europe and America.

- **Data Coverage:** The dataset contains sales orders from **1996 to 1998**, with detailed information about products, categories, customers, employees, and shipping.

- **Overall Sales:** The company generated approximately **$1.35M in total sales** across **829 orders**.

---

## 2. Identifying Business Deficits

The analysis revealed three major business problems that significantly affect the company's profitability:

### 2.1 Severe Profit Deficit

- Despite generating approximately **$1.35M in total sales**, the company recorded an overall **net loss of approximately $94.78K**.
- The overall **profit margin was approximately -7%**, indicating that sales volume does not translate into profitability.

### 2.2 High Freight Costs & Excessive Discounts

- **Freight Costs:** Shipping expenses are significantly high and have a major negative impact on overall profitability.
- **Discounts:** Some products are sold at prices that are lower than their costs due to excessive discounts, resulting in losses on individual sales.

### 2.3 Underperforming Products

- Products such as **Genen Shouyu, Chocolade, and Laughing Lumberjack** have very low sales volumes.
- At the same time, these products generate **net losses on individual transactions**, making them consistently underperforming products.

---

## 3. Strategic Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actionable recommendations can help improve the company's financial performance:

### 3.1 Reduce Shipping Costs

- Renegotiate contracts with shipping companies such as **ShipVia**.
- Alternatively, transfer part of the shipping cost to customers instead of having the company absorb the full cost.

### 3.2 Introduce Discount Guardrails

- Implement a smart discount control system that prevents sales employees from applying discounts that reduce the profit margin below an acceptable threshold.

### 3.3 Address Loss-Making Products

- Review or discontinue consistently loss-making products such as **Laughing Lumberjack**.
- Shift the focus toward stronger-performing categories such as **Beverages** and **Dairy Products**.

### 3.4 Focus on VIP Customer Retention

- Focus on retaining the company's three largest customers: **QUICK-Stop, Save-a-lot, and Ernst Handel**.
- These customers collectively generate more than **$346K** in sales, making their retention important for maintaining a significant portion of the company's total sales.
